Objectives

To improve access to essential oral health services among schoolchildren through systematic screening, oral health education, preventive interventions, primary treatment and referral.


The programme aimed to identify common oral diseases early, provide need-based care at school level, strengthen oral health awareness and support the Government's NSNOP school-health initiative through coordinated, community-oriented dental services.

07 • Sustainability

Designed for Continuity

The programme is integrated within the Government's NSNOP school-health framework.

Government Integration

Formal Government coordination and school-level access provide a framework for continued outreach.

Repeatable Model

Screening, education, prevention, treatment and referral can be systematically repeated across schools.

Follow-up

Continuing reporting and evaluation provide a mechanism for programme improvement.

Re-screening

Periodic re-screening can help identify changing oral health needs.

Referral Completion

Referral follow-up can strengthen continuity between school screening and dental care.

Geo-tagged Register

Maintaining the geo-tagged camp register creates a reproducible record of programme coverage.
